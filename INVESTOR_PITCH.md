## 🎯 Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)

**Eco Nojin** is an intelligent platform that democratizes access to agricultural science
for the world's 2.5 billion smallholder farmers. Through 5 access channels (Web, PWA,
USSD, SMS, Voice) in 14 languages, we deliver peer-reviewed agricultural intelligence to
farmers that no other AgTech platform can reach. Our blockchain-verified carbon credit
registry and AI-powered advisory system help farmers adapt to climate change while
earning from ecosystem restoration. We're raising $500K seed to pilot with 1,000 farmers
and scale to 10M across the MENA region.

## 📊 Problem

### The Digital Divide in Agriculture

| Challenge | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| **2.5B** smallholder farmers worldwide | Largest underserved market |
| **70%** lack digital AgTech tools | Knowledge never reaches them |
| **80%** of platforms require smartphones | Excludes feature phone users |
| **25%** productivity decline by 2050 | Climate change threatens food security |

**Key Insight**: Existing AgTech platforms serve large commercial farms. Smallholders
with basic phones and limited literacy are completely ignored.

## 💡 Solution

### Eco Nojin: A Scientific Platform for All

**Core Engine**: HyDroMa (Hydrology & Drought Monitoring)

**5 Access Channels** (unique in market):
1. 🖥️ **Web App**: Full interactive dashboard
2. 📱 **PWA**: Offline-capable mobile app
3. 📞 **USSD** `*384*73#`: Feature phones (no internet)
4. 💬 **SMS**: Command-based (`SOIL 36.8 54.4`)
5. 🎙️ **Voice IVR**: Low-literacy users

**Scientific Foundation**:
- AI-powered Q&A with peer-reviewed citations (FAO, IPCC, CMIP6)
- Satellite field analysis (Sentinel-2 imagery)
- Climate scenario modeling (SSP pathways)
- Blockchain-verified carbon credits
- Crop yield prediction (8+ crops)
