**Scope.** Every integer composition of the USDA texture triangle
(`sand + silt + clay == 100`), i.e. compositions produced by
`sand in 0..100`, `silt in 0..100-sand`, `clay = 100-sand-silt`:
**5151** compositions. The classifier is the engine's own:
`classify_texture(sand, silt, clay)` from `engine/hydroma/soil/texture.py`,
which delegates to `engine/hydroma/soil/taxonomy.py:_determine_texture_class`.
Nothing here reimplements the classifier, and nothing here changes it.
The tool is stdlib-only and third-party-free: no external
texture-classifier package, no plotting library, no network. The audit is
therefore reproducible without a licence review -- a free replacement
starts from an exact statement of what the current classifier does, and
that statement is this document.

## 5. What a corrected table would need

This tool is the measurement half of that work. It establishes that 782
of 5151 integer compositions (~15 %) get a guess
rather than a classification, that 555 compositions are claimed by more
than one class range, and exactly where those regions are. It deliberately
does **not** touch the classifier: no line of
`engine/hydroma/soil/taxonomy.py` or `engine/hydroma/soil/texture.py` was
edited, and no boundary was invented here.

A corrected table needs the **USDA public-domain** triangle boundaries
themselves: the twelve published class regions of the USDA-NRCS soil texture
triangle (the same public-domain source the twelve range boxes in
`TEXTURE_CLASSES` are already transcribed from), transcribed into the
repository as machine-readable reference data and cited, exactly as
`tests/reference_data/fao56_examples.{csv,md,meta.json}` and
`tests/reference_data/mckee_spi_classification.{csv,meta.json}` carry the
FAO-56 and McKee data with a `README.md` and a `.meta.json` provenance file.
The boundaries must close the triangle (a partition, no gaps) and must not
overlap, so the resolution order stops being load-bearing; the boxes are a
boxification of that diagram, not the diagram.

Acquisition is a data task, not a coding task: the published USDA-NRCS
texture triangle is public domain, but the repository holds no copy of it, and
inventing boundaries here would be a new classifier rather than a repair. Once
the boundaries are in `tests/reference_data/`, the full-grid range-box test in
this tool -- pointed at the transcribed boundaries instead of
`TEXTURE_CLASSES` -- is the harness that verifies the new table over all
5151 compositions, and the 782 and 555 counts above are the
acceptance criteria.
