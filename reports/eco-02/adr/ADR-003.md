# ADR-003: AI Isolation Principle

## Status
Accepted

## Context
AI/ML components are experimental and must not compromise the reproducibility of the scientific core. The chain Engine → Evidence → Decision → AI → User must not be bypassed.

## Decision
- Classify AI as RESEARCH/OPTIONAL only
- AI modules CANNOT import from `engine/` or `c++/`
- All AI inputs must carry Evidence Engine provenance tags
- AI outputs pass through Decision Engine (Tasmimyar) for validation
- Implement import guards in AI modules

## Consequences
- Prevents AI from corrupting scientific computations
- Evidence Engine validates all AI inputs
- Decision Engine filters and validates AI outputs
- Clear separation between experimental AI and verified science

## Violation Detection
- ECO-AI-001: Direct engine import → BLOCK immediately
- ECO-AI-002: Model parameter modification → ROLLBACK
- ECO-AI-003: Bypass of Evidence Engine → REJECT
