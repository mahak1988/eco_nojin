# ADR-009: Research Gaps Registry

## Status
Accepted

## Context
The project has 22+ identified research gaps spanning climate science, ET estimation, crop coefficients, and socio-economic factors. Without tracking, gaps are forgotten.

## Decision
Maintain a registry of research gaps with priority classification:
- **P0**: Critical — blocks progress
- **P1**: High — affects accuracy
- **P2**: Medium — improvements needed
- **P3**: Low — nice to have

### Critical Gaps
| Gap | Priority | Owner |
|-----|----------|-------|
| Climate model downscaling methods | P0 | Climate scientist |
| ET estimation under data-scarce conditions | P0 | Hydrologist |
| Iranian crop coefficients | P1 | Agronomist |
| Groundwater depletion rates | P1 | Hydrogeologist |

## Consequences
- Systematic gap tracking
- Assigned ownership and deadlines
- Integration with Evidence Engine for validation
- Review Queue prioritization
