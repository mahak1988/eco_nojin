# ADR-012: Scientific Review Queue

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Scientific claims must be reviewed before being classified as VERIFIED. No systematic review process exists.

## Decision
Establish a 4-priority review queue:
- **P0**: Critical violations (CONFLICT-006, UNKNOWN-003)
- **P1**: High impact gaps (GAP-003, GAP-004)
- **P2**: Medium impact (GAP-006, GAP-009)
- **P3**: Low impact (GAP-013, GAP-014)

### Review Process
1. Evidence Collection (provenance tags)
2. Peer Review (2+ reviewers)
3. Decision (APPROVE/REJECT/REQUEST_CHANGES)
4. Documentation (update registries)

## Consequences
- Systematic validation of scientific claims
- Priority-based review scheduling
- Clear ownership and deadlines
- Integration with Evidence Engine
