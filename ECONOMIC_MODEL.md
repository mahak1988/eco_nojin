## Executive Summary

Eco Nojin operates as an **investment facilitation platform**, not a recipient of
investment. Investors fund **real agricultural projects** owned by farmers, and the
platform earns sustainable commissions while creating measurable social and environmental impact.

### Key Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Platform Commission | 10% of investment + 5% of profits |
| Investor Expected ROI | 15-40% annually |
| Farmer Net Income Increase | 50-100% |
| De-risk Coverage | 70-90% via insurance + collateral |
| Break-even Point | Month 8-10 |
| 5-Year Revenue Target | $7.5M annually |

## Revenue Streams (Detailed)

### 1. Investment Facilitation Fee

**When**: At time of investment
**Rate**: 10% of total investment amount
**Example**: $10,000 investment = $1,000 platform fee

**Justification**:
- Project evaluation and due diligence
- Satellite and scientific assessment
- Risk scoring and insurance arrangement
- Smart contract deployment
- Legal documentation

### 2. Management Fee

**When**: Annually (or at project completion)
**Rate**: 5% of project profits
**Example**: $4,000 profit = $200 management fee

**Justification**:
- Ongoing monitoring (satellite, IoT, reports)
- Technical advisory to farmers
- Market access facilitation
- Investor reporting
- Insurance claims management

### 3. Marketplace Commission

**When**: On product sales through platform
**Rate**: 2-5% of transaction value
**Example**: $500 sale = $10-25 commission

**Justification**:
- Buyer-seller matching
- Quality verification
- Payment processing
- Logistics coordination
- Traceability (blockchain)

### 4. Professional Subscriptions

**When**: Monthly
**Rate**: $10/month per user
**Target**: Cooperatives, NGOs, extension officers

**Features Included**:
- Advanced analytics dashboard
- API access
- Priority support
- Custom reports
- Training materials

### 5. Enterprise API Licenses

**When**: Monthly/Annual
**Rate**: $500-2,000/month
**Target**: Governments, NGOs, research institutions

**Features Included**:
- Full API access
- White-label options
- Data exports
- Custom integrations
- Dedicated support

### 6. Data Insights (Anonymized)

**When**: Quarterly
**Rate**: $10K-50K per dataset
**Target**: Researchers, policymakers, AgTech companies

**Data Types**:
- Crop yield predictions
- Soil health trends
- Climate impact assessments
- Market price forecasts
- Carbon sequestration data

## Financial Formulas

### ROI Calculation

```
ROI = (Net Profit / Initial Investment) × 100

Where:
Net Profit = Total Revenue - Total Costs - Insurance - Platform Fees

Example (Sheep Project):
Initial Investment: $2,000
Total Revenue (after 12 months): $3,800
Total Costs: $1,000 (feed, veterinary, labor)
Insurance: $80 (4% of investment)
Platform Fee: $200 (10% facilitation) + $135 (5% of $2,700 profit)

Net Profit = $3,800 - $1,000 - $80 - $335 = $2,385
ROI = ($2,385 / $2,000) × 100 = 119.25%

Investor Return: $2,000 + ($2,385 × 60%) = $3,431
Farmer Income: $2,385 × 40% = $954
```

### Profit Distribution

```
Total Net Profit = $2,385

Distribution:
- Investor: 60% = $1,431
- Farmer: 40% = $954
- Platform: 5% of profit = $119 (from investor's share)

Net to Investor: $1,431 - $119 = $1,312
Net to Farmer: $954
Platform Total: $200 (facilitation) + $119 (management) = $319
```

### Insurance Premium Calculation

```
Insurance Premium = Investment × Risk Rate × Coverage Factor

Where:
- Risk Rate: 2-5% (based on project type and location)
- Coverage Factor: 0.7-0.9 (70-90% coverage)

Example:
Investment: $2,000
Risk Rate: 4% (medium risk)
Coverage Factor: 0.8 (80% coverage)

Premium = $2,000 × 0.04 × 0.8 = $64
Max Payout = $2,000 × 0.8 = $1,600
```

## Break-Even Analysis

### Fixed Costs (Monthly)

| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Team Salaries (5 people) | $25,000 |
| Infrastructure (servers, tools) | $2,000 |
| Office & Operations | $3,000 |
| Marketing & Outreach | $5,000 |
| Legal & Compliance | $2,000 |
| Insurance Reserve | $3,000 |
| **Total** | **$40,000** |

### Variable Costs (Per Project)

| Item | Cost |
|------|------|
| Due Diligence | $50 |
| Satellite Assessment | $20 |
| Insurance Premium | 3-5% of investment |
| Transaction Fees | 1% of investment |
| Monitoring (annual) | $30 |

### Break-Even Calculation

```
Monthly Fixed Costs: $40,000
Average Revenue per Project: $150 (10% facilitation on $1,000 avg + 5% management)

Projects Needed to Break Even: $40,000 / $150 = 267 projects/month
Investment Volume Needed: 267 × $1,000 = $267,000/month

Timeline: Month 8-10 (based on growth trajectory)
```
