# 00. Master Plan: Eco Nojin & HyDroMa

**Status:** Approved | **Version:** 1.0.0 | **Classification:** Strategic

## 1. Vision
To establish an international, standards-based digital infrastructure that bridges the gap between advanced computational science (Hydrology, Ecology, AI) and rural livelihoods. The platform empowers smallholder farmers, pastoralists, and nomadic communities through precise ecosystem restoration, carbon incentivization, and resilient agricultural practices.

## 2. Core Objectives
1. **Ecological Restoration:** Reversing desertification, restoring watersheds, and enhancing soil organic carbon through data-driven afforestation and bio-fertilizer formulation.
2. **Economic Inclusion:** Providing nomadic and rural populations with digital identity, alternative credit scoring, index-based insurance, and direct access to global carbon and organic markets.
3. **Scientific Rigor:** Operating on a multi-physics simulation engine (HyDroMa) combined with physics-informed machine learning to ensure recommendations are scientifically valid and locally calibrated.
4. **Global Compliance:** Aligning natively with FAO Digital Villages Initiative, UN SDGs, ISO 14064 (Carbon), and OGC Spatial Standards.

## 3. Target Demographics
- **Smallholder Farmers:** Seeking yield optimization, water efficiency, and climate resilience.
- **Pastoralists & Nomads:** Requiring dynamic migration routing, forage monitoring, and livestock insurance.
- **Rural Youth & Women:** Seeking gig-economy employment, ecotourism opportunities, and local processing enterprises.
- **Governments & NGOs:** Requiring MRV (Measurement, Reporting, and Verification) tools for national climate commitments.

## 4. Phased Execution Strategy
- **Phase 1 (Research & MVP):** Core soil, water, and crop simulation modules. Local storage (SQLite/DuckDB).
- **Phase 2 (Ecosystem & Materials):** Biochar, compost formulation, and small-scale watershed structures.
- **Phase 3 (Risk & Crisis):** Drought/Flood early warning systems and index-based insurance triggers.
- **Phase 4 (Inclusion & Economy):** Nomad routing, marketplace, micro-credit, and internal reward tokens.
- **Phase 5 (Global Scale):** Carbon credit verification, blockchain ledger, post-quantum security, and international API integrations.
